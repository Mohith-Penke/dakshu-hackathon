05 / AWARENESS

Know the Warning Signs

Common patterns that deserve extra verification.

💳

Payment Requests

Be cautious when a recruiter asks for registration, training, security or processing fees.

💰

Unrealistic Salary

Extremely high income promises with little experience or effort deserve verification.

⏰

Urgency

Pressure to pay or respond immediately can prevent proper verification.

🔐

Sensitive Data

Never share OTPs, UPI PINs, passwords or banking credentials with recruiters.
