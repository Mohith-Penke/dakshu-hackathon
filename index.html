SCAM AWARENESS

Common Job Scam Signals

Fraudulent job offers often use pressure, money, unrealistic promises, or sensitive information requests.

💳

Payment Requests

Requests for registration fees, training fees, security deposits, or other payments before joining.

HIGH RISK SIGNAL
💰

Unrealistic Salary

Extremely high or guaranteed earnings with little experience, qualifications, or interview process.

CHECK CAREFULLY
⏱️

Urgency

Pressure to respond immediately or accept an offer before verifying the company.

WARNING SIGNAL
🔐

Sensitive Data

Requests for OTPs, bank details, UPI PINs, Aadhaar, PAN, card information, or similar credentials.

PROTECT YOUR DATA

SAFETY CENTER

Stay Safe Before You Accept

Always independently verify an employer before sharing information or making any payment.

🛡️

Job Safety Checklist

Verify these points before proceeding.

✓

Verify the company independently using its official website.

✓

Make sure no payment is required to receive the job.

✓

Look for a clear and legitimate interview process.

✓

Check whether salary and responsibilities are realistic.

✓

Never share OTPs, UPI PINs, passwords, or banking credentials.

🔎

Verify Before You Trust

A low risk score does not guarantee a job offer is genuine.

Company Search for the company's official website, domain, address, and public presence.
Recruiter Verify the recruiter's identity and relationship with the company.
Communication Be cautious with unexpected WhatsApp, Telegram, or personal-email recruitment.
Documents Never send sensitive identity or financial information without legitimate verification.
🚨

Already Lost Money to a Job Scam?

Preserve messages, payment records, screenshots, phone numbers, email addresses, and other evidence. Contact your bank or payment provider immediately and use official cybercrime reporting channels.

ℹ️

Important Disclaimer

AI JobGuard provides a rule-based risk assessment based on patterns detected in the submitted text. It is not 100% accurate and cannot guarantee that an opportunity is genuine or fraudulent. A low-risk result does not guarantee legitimacy. Always verify the employer independently before sharing sensitive information, making payments, or accepting an offer.
